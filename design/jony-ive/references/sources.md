# Source ledger and research notes

Research snapshot: **28 October 2026**. IDs A01–D20 preserve the order and all
**84 entries** in the user's supplied library: 28 interviews/talks, 25 product
studies, 11 recordings, and 20 books/independent sources. The supplied text contained
titles and annotations, not the promised 84 URLs or full source texts. URLs below
were independently located; an unresolved entry is retained rather than invented.

## How to use this evidence

- **Consulted:** relevant original text, transcript, or reported passages were
  read. This does not assert every word, image, or embedded film was reviewed.
- **Partial:** only an accessible excerpt, search-indexed passage, or supplied
  reporting was consulted. Use only the stated detail; inspect more for new claims.
- **Located:** the source or metadata was found, but substantive content was not
  reviewed. A viewing/reading lead, not evidence of its detailed arguments.
- **Unresolved:** an exact source/version could not be established. The supplied
  annotation is a research lead, not verified knowledge.
- **Repeated:** overlaps another entry; do not count it as independent corroboration.

“Primary” means an original statement or project source, not impartial evaluation.
Client announcements and launch films are interested sources. Source years may
refer to an event or original issue rather than a later web republication.
No full books were read for this package. No unavailable recording is represented
as watched. Notes are paraphrases; the exercises and modern applications in the
other files are the skill author's synthesis.

For attribution, name the source as well as its ID. An ID alone is an internal
navigation aid. Do not infer personal authorship of a team decision from a product
appearing in this bibliography. Do not treat these entries as 84 independent
experiments validating a coherent theory.
